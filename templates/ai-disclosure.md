# Generic AI-Assisted Writing Disclosure Template

> **Important Note**: Editorial requirements regarding generative AI and automated writing, translation, or illustration tools vary across academic publishers and evolve over time. This document is a **general-purpose disclosure template** and does not represent the policy of any specific publisher. Always consult your target press or acquisitions editor for their current submission requirements before finalizing your manuscript.

---

## 2. Short Disclosure Statements (For Preface, Acknowledgments, or Front Matter)

> Choose the option below that accurately reflects your workflow, delete unused options, and adapt bracketed fields.

### Option A: Copy-Editing, Formatting & Language Polishing Only
> During the preparation of this manuscript, the author(s) used `[Tool/Model Name(s)]` strictly for `[language polishing, grammar checking, and formatting assistance]` on text written by the author(s). No generative AI tool was used to formulate scholarly arguments, analyze data, or generate bibliographic citations. The author(s) reviewed and edited all text and take full responsibility for the content of this publication.

### Option B: Structural Drafting or Translation Assistance Grounded in Author Materials
> During the preparation of this book, the author(s) used `[Tool/Model Name(s)]` to assist with `[chapter outline refinement / prose drafting from author-prepared research notes / initial cross-language translation]` in `[specify chapters or "selected sections"]`. All conceptual frameworks, analytical conclusions, and bibliographic selections were determined by the author(s). Every factual claim, quotation, equation, and reference was independently verified against primary sources and substantially revised by the author(s), who assume full responsibility for the integrity and accuracy of the work.

### Option C: Code, Data Processing, or Schematic Illustration Assistance
> In preparing `[specify chapters, figures, or companion code]`, the author(s) used `[Tool/Model Name(s)]` to assist with `[drafting data-processing scripts / generating initial conceptual diagram layouts]`. All code outputs were executed and validated by the author(s), and all visual elements were inspected and edited for scientific accuracy and copyright compliance. The author(s) take full responsibility for the final manuscript and accompanying materials.
